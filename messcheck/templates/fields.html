Choosing a field type

  • checkbox
    Verified or not verified. Only these count towards a record's compliance score. Use them for anything an inspector can confirm at a glance.
  • text
    A short written answer. Good for a supplier name or the date of the last pest control visit. Recorded, but not scored.
  • number
    A measurement. A fridge temperature, a bin count. Recorded, but not scored.

Every field carries a comment box on the form, whatever its type, so an inspector can always add context.

Categories group the form into sections. Typing a category that does not exist yet creates it.
