About {{ app_name }}

Why this exists and how a weekly check works.

Start a new check

{{ app_name }} is the food safety checklist used for the {{ org_name }} facilities. It replaces the paper form that used to circulate between inspectors, so every visit is recorded in one place and can be compared with the previous one.

How a check works

  1. 1

    Open the form

    Go to New check and confirm the date, time and your name.

  2. 2

    Walk the categories

    Tick what you verified and leave a comment on anything that needs follow-up.

  3. 3

    Submit

    The record is saved with a compliance score: the share of checkbox items you were able to verify.

  4. 4

    Revisit or export

    Anyone can reopen the record later to correct it, or export it as CSV for a report.

The checklist is not fixed

The questions live in Fields. Add a field when a new risk needs watching, reorder them so the form follows your walking route, or retire one that no longer applies.

Changes apply to the next inspection. Records already saved keep the answers they were given.

What the score means

The score counts only checkbox items. A field asking for a temperature or a date is recorded but not scored, because there is no single correct answer to count.

  • 80% and above on track
  • 50% to 79% worth a conversation with the mess manager
  • below 50% flagged in red across the app

Who can use it

There are no accounts. Anyone who can reach the site can record a check, edit a record, or change the field list, so it is meant for an internal network or a trusted group.

Deleting a record asks you to retype the inspector's name first, which is the one guard against an accidental click.

Questions

Reach the mess committee through the contact page. For anything urgent found between visits, email rather than waiting for the next weekly check.
